How to write text:

The candidate reads your text on the role cards and in the mission report, and does not see your task. This includes the
values that you save, notes, reasons, evidence, and the gaps and notes that you save with save_notes. Write it so that a
person can read it alone:
- Write full, short sentences. Do not join parts of a text with ";".
- Do not put sources or dates in brackets in the text. Say the source in the sentence, for example "LinkedIn showed this
  role on 7 Oct 2026". Put URLs in `sources` when the tool has it.
- In sentences, write dates as "7 Oct 2026".
- Do not use short forms such as "yrs", "w/" or "approx.".
- Never repeat the resume text in a reply, a summary or a note. Use only the fact that you need.
